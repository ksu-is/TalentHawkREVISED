About PCA

Principal Component Analysis (PCA) is a statistical technique that reduces the dimensionality of data while retaining most of the variation.

In soccer analytics, PCA helps identify which combination of attributes best explain player differences and similarities.

Players clustered together in the PCA chart have similar statistical profiles, even if their raw numbers differ.

Player Clustering Analysis
PCA

This chart maps players in a 2D space based on their statistical profile. Players who appear close together have similar playing styles and attributes.

Colors represent different positions, showing how statistical profiles align with traditional positions.

Insight: The principal components represent combinations of statistics that best explain differences between players. Players clustered together may be suitable alternatives for scouting or tactical purposes.
Statistical Insights

Based on the PCA analysis, we can identify several key insights about player types:

  • Forwards typically score high on goals, shots on target, and sprint speed.
  • Midfielders excel in passing accuracy, distance covered, and assists.
  • Defenders show strong values in tackles, interceptions, and aerial duels.

These patterns help scouts identify players who may be able to play multiple positions or those who have unique attribute combinations that could provide tactical advantages.
